## Build order and the 8+ bar

1. **First playable slice:** ship the completed-room card, the first album page and event, and the single-link dare through landing and reply. That tests both loops end to end with existing art.
2. **Then build out the calendar and relay:** load all 24 weeks, add the Daily milestones and rotating requests, ship the Street overlays, and finish the clip URL, QR, and pass-along flow.
3. **Score the result on visible evidence and actual handoffs.** For retention, capture a completed-house save on an ordinary day, after a claim, after a missed day, at weekly rollover, and at weeks 4, 12, and 24. Each state needs a visible next action, a specific payoff, and a reason to return. For virality, run sender → received message → first move → reply on **iPhone/iMessage, iPhone/WhatsApp, and Android/WhatsApp**. Test a clipboard refusal and a received file with its caption removed. Require every supported route to reach the exact dare from one message or from the link burned into the file.
