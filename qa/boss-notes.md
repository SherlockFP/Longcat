# How many levels? (R11 boss lane)

There is no last level: `src/core/generate.js` builds every level from its number on the fly (seeded, so L250 is the same board for everyone), and `scripts/check-core.mjs` proves L1-600 solvable under the real rules. Designed ranges: L1-4 are hand-made tutorials, L6 / L9 / L11 are curated boards, the teaching levels are L8 dog, L12 yarn, L41 mystery (moved off L40, now a boss), L55 frozen, L61 spring and L81 swivel. From L61 the run is 20-level themed packs (Rainy Day, Bakery Rush, Garden Party, Starry Night). The first lap (L61-140) brings in the new pieces; after that, the four themes repeat every 80 levels with no new lessons. Every 20th level is a boss. The 12 named bosses repeat every 240 levels, and the boss chest's rarity cycle repeats every 6 bosses (120 levels). Map chapters (10 levels each) have 12 hand-named stops, then 144 generated names up to L1560, and then the names repeat. Difficulty stops climbing at about L100-250: board size tops out from L50, fill at L100 and dependency depth at L250. After that, new boards are fresh but no harder.

Old dare links: the boss lane moved the hard levels, so the level schedule is versioned. `generate(n, 1)` is the schedule before it (hard every 7-10 from L15, super hard about every 30, mystery on L40), byte for byte; `generate(n)` is schedule 2 (bosses). The dare token's leading digit carries it: 2 / 3 / v1 links (made before) open the schedule-1 board, 4 (current) the boss one (`src/core/curated.js` boardOf, `scripts/check-dare.mjs`). Boss boards are a giant order: 8x11 at L20, a boss-only 9x11 from L40 (cells 32px at 375x667), 24-27 cats against 15-18 around them.
