**6.5/10 combined.** Long Cat Out! has one genuinely fun move: send the right cat, finish a carrier, and watch three more cats jump off the bench. The cats have personality, and the finished rooftop earns the café its screen time. But too much of the puzzle is clearing whichever cat can move until there’s a bench-space test. The café runs out of builds around L60, and the best chain is easier to watch than to challenge a friend to reproduce. I reran the core and economy checks. This score is a design judgment from the frames and code, without player retention or sharing data.

1. **Make the generator reward planning a chain.** All 600 levels pass the checks. On the intended paths through L5–14, 21 of 103 turns had a single legal exit, and on 65 every legal exit still left a possible win. Fourteen of the 17 losing alternatives overflowed the bench immediately. Teach players to set something up. Require at least one fork on a normal board where both exits are survivable, but one frees a key lane or sets up a three-cat hop within the next two taps. Score those forks across several viable paths. Right now the band scores traps and chains along one scripted path.
