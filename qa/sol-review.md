Hand someone the Level 25 frame and ask them to point in each cat’s exit direction. Every hesitation is a UI bug.

2. **Make Level 3 prove why kittens matter.** The spec places the mechanic at both Level 3 and Level 6. Commit to Level 3. Build a five-second chain: the kitten blocks a third cat, the parent visibly pulls the kitten out, and the third cat is now free. Give both linked cats an obvious matching collar with a shared symbol, then pulse the newly freed cat’s exit chevron. In the current frame, I see overlapping cats and paw prints. I should see a cause and an effect.

3. **Make bonks teach the player what blocked them.** The stars in Level 6 show which cat got dizzy. Briefly trace its attempted exit, flash the first occupied tile, then drain the heart. Keep the feedback quick enough that players can immediately try the move they just figured out. A player should be able to answer “What stopped me?” after one bonk.
