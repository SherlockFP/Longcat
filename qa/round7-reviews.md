## critic 5
- [high] Generated levels have almost no real decisions, and difficulty goes down over time. I traced the solver on each winning line. L12: 10 taps and 30 alternative options. Only 1 option loses later (s2: tapping the green pair early). The other 3 losing options are instant bench overflows, which is plain arithmetic. L30: 13 taps. The only planning mistake is 'mint pair early', offered 3 turns in a row (s3-s5), so it is one decision. L60 (8x11, 18 cats): 15 taps, dead ratio 0.044, and 1 lose-later option in about 45. It is easier than L12 (0.133) and L30 (0.152). Over 90% of taps win whatever you pick, so players do not need to think.
  FIX: Drop the generator's trap acceptance from d.traps to d.late, so instant-overflow options stop counting as traps. Require late >= 2 at distinct steps (different units) from L10, and >= 3 from L40. Add a hard floor so lateRatio rises with level: L10-39 >= 0.05, L40+ >= 0.08. Reject boards where the same unit is the only trap across consecutive steps.
- [high] Every level ends the same way. In all 3 traces the bench climbs to peak 4, a [3,1] double chain fires, and then 2-5 taps follow with free=1 (L12 s8-9, L30 s9-12, L60 s10-14). check-core shows peak 4 and bench 5 in every range from L5 to L500. So the chain, the one clip moment, always happens on a nearly empty board (28f/28g: 'Chain x3!' over an empty grid), and the last third of each level is forced cleanup.
  FIX: Have the generator place the big chain in the middle of the line, at about 40-70% of taps with at least 5 cats still on the board. Penalise forced tails: more than 2 trailing free=1 taps = reject. Vary bench size per level kind (4 on hard, 6 on breather levels) so the peak target is not always 4/5.
- [high] Mechanics are sparse in mid-game. L30 and L60 have zero dog, yarn, mystery or frozen pieces. check-core shows L12-39 with 19 pieces over 28 levels, and L55-69 with 21 over 15. Frozen is taught at L55, and the very next sample, L60, has none. The dog in the teaching level (24c/24e) only wakes when the board is nearly empty, so it never changed the solution.
  FIX: From each teach level, carry the newest mechanic into at least 2 of the next 4 levels. Require each mechanic to sit on the winning line before step N/2, which means its removal unblocks a cat that is needed early. Reject a teach board if the dog or frozen cat clears in the last 30% of taps.

## code 6
- [high] src/kit/ads.js line 54: the line `const PLAY_CEILING_MS = 90000;` was merged into a comment, so PLAY_CEILING_MS is never defined. It is read at line 64 (`started()` in h5Rewarded) and line 90 (`beforeAd` in h5Interstitial). With VITE_ADS_PROVIDER=h5 (the planned release config), `beforeReward` calls `started()` before `showAd()`, so it throws a ReferenceError and the rewarded ad is never shown. Every rewarded action (+1 seat, +1 life, hint) ends in the 9 s start timeout and 'No ad right now'. The interstitial `beforeAd` throws the same way, which leaves audio.hold(true) set with no ceiling timer. `npm run build` and `npm run check` both pass, because nothing exercises the h5 path.
  FIX: Restore `const PLAY_CEILING_MS = 90000;` on its own line. Add a check that loads ads.js with provider h5 and a stubbed adBreak, then fires beforeReward and beforeAd.
- [low] src/main.js guarded() releases busy after 20 s, but the h5 play ceiling is 90 s. While a long rewarded ad is still running, a second guarded action (adlife, adheart, hint) can start. When both ads resolve, adlife can add two lives; hint can spend one ad for two hints.
  FIX: Make the guarded release timeout at least PLAY_CEILING_MS + the start timeout, or key a separate in-flight flag per ad type.
- [low] src/game/game.js destOf() runs on every press: it calls rules tap() then rulesUndo(st, true). tap() calls snapshot(), which shifts the oldest entry once undo.length >= UNDO_MAX (60). The dry-run undo only pops the newest entry, so each press on a long level (more than 60 moves) drops one real undo step.
  FIX: Take the preview on a structuredClone of the state, or pass a no-snapshot flag to tap(). Alternatively, restore the shifted entry after the dry run.
- [low] src/kit/clip.js mark(): frames of the replaced best clip that are not in the ring are recycled into the pool. A running encode() of the old best still reads them, and grab() then overwrites them, which corrupts the shared video. Rare, because encode normally starts only after the win.
  FIX: Do not recycle old.frames while `job` is pending, or bump a generation counter and abort the old encode.
