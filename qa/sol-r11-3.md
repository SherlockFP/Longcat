### Highest-impact fixes, all client-side

1. **Presentation.** On mobile, make the bottom **Play** the primary start button and use the active map node as a level marker. Give the resident goal room for its full action and reserve clear space for the front carrier’s count below the plaque. Check the map, teaching board, and win card at 360×640 and 375×667 in English and Turkish.
2. **Appeal.** Apply the tabby’s body profile and integrated head-to-shoulder join to pink, blue, and green. Fit each breed’s markings to the changing width so they wrap around bends. Judge the pass on a crowded board at actual phone size.
3. **Juice.** Profile a full-bench chain on a low-end Android first. If raster is the bottleneck, put moving cats and transient effects on a lower-resolution layer while keeping still cats and the HUD crisp. Preserve overlapping taps and landing timing. Use a 33 ms p95 frame interval on that phone as the first acceptance target.
4. **Fun.** Before the L9 pair commits, highlight the **two cushions** it would occupy and connect them to the last green carrier. If that move causes the first loss, let the teaching retry rewind to the pair’s tap. Watch fresh players attempt L6 and L9 and check whether they can explain their next choice unaided.
5. **Retention.** Make the nearest album reward actionable from the map once the early resident goal ends. Show one specific postcard, the exact board or stamp left, and a button that opens it. When a page is complete, send the player straight to the room to hang the print.
6. **Virality.** Put the production HTTPS dare link in the primary share path and make the captioned QR still available immediately alongside the optional video. Test the received message through first move and counter-dare on iMessage and WhatsApp, including a video forwarded without its caption.
7. **Overall.** Playtest one uninterrupted opening on phones, from the first chain through the first resident build and the first dare offer. Use the existing local counters and observed play to find where players stop, then fix that handoff before adding more modes or rewards.
