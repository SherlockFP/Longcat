### Most important remaining fixes

1. **Presentation.** At 375 × 667, collapse the completed-floor rail into a single house picker on Cat Street. Move shop names into a dedicated row below the façades and use the recovered road space to keep both goal tiles and Play visible.
2. **Appeal.** Start with the orange tabby. Widen the shoulders under the head, round the belly, taper the rump, and wrap the stripes around those contours. Check it at board size on both straight cats and tight bends before rolling it out to other breeds.
3. **Juice.** Give each landing a visible contact beat. Hold the paws briefly on the carrier lip, compress the body into the opening, and let the carrier settle with a small dip. Rebound each cushion as its cat launches during a chain. Fit these reactions into the current timing and preserve overlapping taps.
4. **Fun.** Turn the first full-bench loss into a playable lesson. Pulse the needed carrier or its queue badge and show how many departures away it is. After **Take back**, briefly mark the restored free cushion and one cat that can board immediately, where available. Check whether fresh players change their next choice without a prompt.
5. **Retention.** Shorten the wait for the first framed print. Make one page completable within the first four event weeks, let players use their weekly archive claim on any already-released postcard, and show the exact board or release date in each missing slot.
6. **Virality.** Add a scannable QR for the public HTTPS dare URL to the end card and fallback PNG. Verify send → received message → first move → reply on iPhone/iMessage, iPhone/WhatsApp, and Android/WhatsApp, including a clip stripped of its caption.
